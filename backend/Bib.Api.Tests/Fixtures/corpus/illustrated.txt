# The Garden Notebook
This notebook records the first year of the garden behind the old school. Each page combines a short note with a picture taken on the same day.
[img:pattern.png]
In March the soil was still cold, but the first green shoots appeared along the southern wall. We planted beans, peas and two rows of carrots.
[img:sample.jpg]
By June the beans had climbed the fence and the carrots were ready to thin. The children counted forty-seven ladybirds on a single afternoon.
# Autumn
The harvest was smaller than hoped, but nobody minded. We kept the best seeds in paper envelopes, labelled them carefully and stored them in the cupboard for next year.
